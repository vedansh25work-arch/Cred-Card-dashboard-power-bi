# Cred-Card-dashboard-power-bi
📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Credit Card Financial Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze customer demographics, transaction behavior, revenue generation, and spending patterns.

The dashboard transforms raw credit card transaction data into meaningful business insights through dynamic visualizations, KPI cards, slicers, and drill-down analysis. It enables stakeholders to monitor customer performance, identify revenue-driving segments, and make data-driven business decisions.

The project consists of two fully interactive dashboards:

Customer Report Dashboard
Transaction Report Dashboard
🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze customer demographics and financial behavior.
Track revenue generated across different customer segments.
Understand transaction patterns and spending habits.
Compare revenue across card categories.
Identify high-value customer groups.
Build an interactive business intelligence solution for decision-making.

🔑 Key Business Insights
Customer Insights:
Blue Card customers contribute the highest overall revenue.
Customers aged 40–60 years generate the maximum revenue.
Graduate customers contribute the highest revenue among all education levels.
Businessmen and White-collar professionals are the top revenue-generating occupations.
High-income customers contribute significantly more revenue than low-income customers.
Customers with 2–3 dependents generate the highest revenue.
Texas, New York, and California are among the top-performing states.
Male customers contribute slightly more revenue than female customers.
Transaction Insights:
Swipe transactions dominate overall spending.
Bills represent the largest expenditure category.
Entertainment and Fuel are other major spending categories.
Revenue remains relatively stable across all quarters.
Transaction volume peaks during Q3.
Blue Card users account for the majority of total transaction revenue.
Online transactions contribute the least compared to Swipe and Chip transactions.
